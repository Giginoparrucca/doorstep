# Security policy

## Supported deployment

We support the current production release at [app.welcomebnb.it](https://app.welcomebnb.it), built from the main branch of this repository. Demo deployments, third-party platforms and infrastructure belonging to others are outside this testing scope.

## Report privately

Use [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Giginoparrucca/doorstep/security/advisories), or email [info@welcomebnb.it](mailto:info@welcomebnb.it) with the subject “WelcomeBnB security report”. Do not open a public issue containing exploitation details, credentials or guest information.

Include the affected component, a concise impact explanation, minimal reproduction with synthetic data, and a way to contact you. Redact tokens, booking links, access codes, documents and personal data. We aim to acknowledge reports within 48 hours and provide progress updates while investigating.

The dedicated security@welcomebnb.it alias is not yet verified; use the published address above until this policy explicitly announces the alias.

## Good-faith research

Use accounts and properties you control. Keep testing minimal and stop if you encounter someone else's data. Do not download or retain it; report the access path privately. Do not alter bookings, submit real guest registrations, trigger messages, payments or external integrations, exhaust resources, or disrupt service. Ask us to coordinate any test that could affect a real host or guest.

We consider good-faith research that follows this scope authorized and will work with you to resolve the report. This does not authorize testing third parties or accessing other people's data, and no bounty is promised.

## Coordinated disclosure

Please allow up to 90 days for coordinated remediation and disclosure. We will discuss a suitable timeline with the reporter, including earlier action when a vulnerability is being exploited. Publish only after coordination and without personal data or working credentials.
